# Field Sales Force Automation (FSFA)

## Overview
A field-sales solution concept designed to support sales representatives, supervisors, and regional management through structured field activities and approval workflows.

## Business Scope
- Sales representatives, teams, routes, and customer visits
- Customer activity tracking
- Sales orders and invoice-related workflows
- Collections and payment follow-up
- Surveys and customer information capture
- Supervisor review and approval processes

## Functional Design Highlights
- Activity-based field workflows
- Required visit outcomes and controlled check-in rules
- Approval paths for selected operational requests
- Role-based access for representatives and supervisors
- Mobile-friendly field user experience
