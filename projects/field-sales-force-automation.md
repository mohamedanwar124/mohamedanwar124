# Field Sales Force Automation (FSFA)

## Project Overview
A mobile-friendly field-sales solution designed to help sales organizations coordinate representative activities, customer visits, sales transactions, and supervisor approvals.

The solution brings key field operations into a structured workflow for sales representatives, supervisors, and regional management.

## Business Challenge
Field-sales operations involve several connected activities: planning customer visits, recording outcomes, processing orders and collections, and escalating requests for review. Without consistent workflows, it can be difficult to maintain visibility and apply the same operating rules across field teams.

## Functional Scope
- Sales representatives, teams, territories, routes, and customer visits
- Visit activities covering sales orders, invoice workflows, collections, returns, stock counts, surveys, and customer information capture
- Role-based workflows for representatives, supervisors, and regional managers
- Supervisor review and approval for selected operational requests
- Customer-related requests routed through an approval process
- Stock and load-request workflows with quantity review
- Mobile-friendly access to daily field activities

## Workflow Controls
- Configurable visit check-in rules and required visit outcomes
- Controlled activity progression and role-based permissions
- Approval steps for requests that require supervisor review
- Supervisor tools to review and close field activities when required

## Business Value
The solution is intended to improve consistency and traceability across field activities, make pending approvals more visible, and give supervisors a clearer view of field execution. No quantified business results are claimed in this case study.
