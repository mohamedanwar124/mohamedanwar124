# Product Cost Analysis & Accounting Reconciliation

## Overview
A reporting approach to analyze product cost using accounting entries and operational quantities, helping finance and management investigate cost movements and reconcile differences.

## Business Scope
- Cost-of-revenue journal entry analysis
- Sales and return movement distinction
- Cost per unit calculations
- Monthly and company-level comparison
- Trend indicators and exception investigation

## Functional Design Highlights
- Traceable accounting data sources
- Clear treatment of sales versus returns
- Period and company filters
- Reconciliation-oriented reporting

## My Contribution
Requirement analysis, accounting logic clarification with stakeholders, report design, and functional validation.
