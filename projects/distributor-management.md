# Distributor Management

## Overview
A distributor-management solution concept for managing stock movement and periodic financial settlement between a company and its distributors.

## Business Scope
- Distributor classification and operational setup
- Dedicated warehouse and stock locations
- Transfers to distributor locations
- Partial receipt and quantity reconciliation
- Period-end sales and settlement processing
- Reporting for outstanding quantities and balances

## Functional Design Highlights
- Traceable stock movement
- Controlled receipt and discrepancy handling
- Separation of distributor operations from standard customer receivables where required
- Periodic settlement workflow aligned with accounting policy
